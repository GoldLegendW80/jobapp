## Decisions

**Owner decisions, taken on 2026-10-14** (each is a decision, with the owner's words; the full text is in SPEC-apply).
1. **Full auto: "Guarded defaults".** Off until the person turns it on; then only postings at or above the person's fit threshold, one application per company in 30 days, a hold of at least 15 minutes with Cancel before each submit, Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever forms first (e-mail after A14, LinkedIn Easy Apply after A16), and the first 3 runs as rehearsals (filled, screenshotted, not submitted).
2. **Daily limit: "By fit score only".** The fit threshold `apply_settings.min_fit` (0 to 100, the range of documents' `search.documents.min_fit`) decides which postings Full auto applies to. Three thresholds meet in the app and each card names its own: "Show postings from" (`search.min_fit`, on main, default 70), "Write documents from" (`search.documents.min_fit`) and "Apply automatically from" (`apply_settings.min_fit`). No fixed daily number: a safety ceiling of 30 submissions a day, in Advanced settings, is a runaway backstop only (the rest goes to the review queue). The fixed 10 a day is removed.
3. **Accounts on job sites.** "The agent open playwright, user sign in or log in, we save all the playwright profile per user so next opening will be connected. User can ask to complete his profile on the website with all informations." The app never chooses a password or creates an account; the person signs in or signs up himself in the live view (browser **B12a** and **B12b**, used by **A10b** and **A15**); the profile is persisted per person (browser B2, B3, B7); and **A18a to A18c** are the action "Complete my profile on this site".
4. **LinkedIn: "Full use".** Searches (browser B10) and Easy Apply (**A16**), in Full auto too, from the person's own signed in session, with human pacing, a low request rate and the per company and per day guards; a per person switch, off by default, with one sentence about LinkedIn's terms accepted once (browser B10).

**Owner answers, taken on 2026-10-19** (issue #405; the index is [docs/decisions.md](../../docs/decisions.md)).
1. **Full auto first, without the sign in.** Full auto ships first for Ashby, Greenhouse and Lever, before browser B12 and the sign in half of A10 (**A10b**); until then a sign in wall sends the item to the review queue as "Needs you".
2. **The check by model is apply's own:** one model call per item (**A13c**, about 6,500 tokens), since mail M20 is paused; until it lands, an item with free text written by the agent goes to the review queue.
3. **A rehearsal answered Yes** lands in the review queue, ready for a one click Approve.
4. **`form_changed` hands over to the generic form agent** by itself in both modes (Review first and Full auto), within the run's token budget and every guardrail (the gate, the caps, the safety ceiling, the check by model before a submit). This answers #343's question.
5. **Planner default, not asked** (the owner may change it): an approval or a Full auto Like that only the pace per host holds back stays valid, at most 7 days after it was given, and the gate checks everything again before the click (A13a); a Full auto Like stays valid at most 7 days in any case, and only while Full auto stays on.

**Owner answer, taken on 2026-10-21** (issue #464): “no limit.” Likes have no per-click, session or daily cap. Application guardrails remain in force, including fit checks, company caps, pacing, holds and the safety ceiling.

**Defaults decided by the planner (2026-10-14)**, which the owner may change.
- No model ever clicks Submit. The one click path is `apply/gate.py`, through the browser's `gate_click` capability (B5), after a decision that writes its audit row first.
- Review first refills from the approved answers in a fresh lease with no model call, reads each value back and refuses on a difference (`form_changed`).
- `submit_unknown` is never resubmitted by the app; "It was submitted" or "Try again" (a new approval) are the person's.
- Term boxes: the employer's privacy notice for this application and "the information is accurate" may be ticked; arbitration, marketing, talent pools, background check authorisations and anything else are the person's.
- EEO: `decline` by default. Salary: `leave_blank` by default. Neither is ever inferred.
- Company caps are the person's own entries (the owner enters the two he knows); the pace per ATS host is global (one submission every 3 minutes, 20 a day; LinkedIn's own pace, one every 10 minutes and 15 a day) because every person shares the Jetson's address.
- Screenshots: "before" and "after" kept as long as the application; step and error images 30 days.
- Full auto's rehearsals and every `dry_run` go through the whole gate and stop before the click.
- Documents never block (their decision): apply stores the fit and the corrections of the versions it sends and shows them; only Full auto uses them (an overflowing version or a non empty `corrections` goes to the review queue). The freeze (`documents.freeze`) is called in the transaction that records the submission, after the click, never at the decision, and never refuses; `submit_unknown` freezes the sent versions too, since the files may have been received.

## Rules every task follows
- **Isolation:** at least one test with two people, A and B, with postings at the same company: B's application, answers, screenshots, approval and answer bank answer 404 to A; A's approval can never authorise B's submission.
- **No submission outside the gate:** every task that adds a path to a form has a test that the fake ATS server received no submission except through `gate.submit`, and one that the agent's tools refuse the submit element.
- **No real company in a test,** ever. The fake ATS server is the only form a test fills; the stub base address is refused in production. The owner's real applications are human steps of Checkpoint A.
- **Never apply:** every task that starts filling or submitting tests a posting whose title matches a never apply rule (the agent never starts; the gate refuses).
- **Canary:** mail M1's `canary.py` over logs, Redis, job arguments, arq results and error answers: no answer value, typed text, screenshot byte or lease token.
- **Evidence** under `tasks/evidence/apply-a<n>/` with the journey's screenshots; figures under "Measurements".
- **Merge rule:** CONTRIBUTING's, with the local check in place of CI; the orchestrator merges.
