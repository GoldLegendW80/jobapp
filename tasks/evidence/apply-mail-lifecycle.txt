Apply email lifecycle review fixes, issue #563
2026-10-22

B1: Send-audit retention
The existing API mail dispatch cycle prunes at most 100 audits per invocation,
including when no account, send key or provider is available. The injected clock
and MAIL_SENDS_KEEP_DAYS define the cutoff; only strictly older audits expire.
An audit survives disconnect and posting deletion until that cutoff. Account
deletion retains its existing cascade. A pending attempt is settled as unknown
before pruning; the application keeps its uncertain state and spent authority.

B2: Local non-send versus provider outcome
The pending audit commits before the provider call. The application remains
approved or held while the final live checks run, then submitting commits before
capability minting. Proven local refusals commit the failed audit and Apply
stop or handover together. Full auto becomes review, the approval stays spent,
and no capability or provider call occurs. Actual provider 400 and unknown
outcomes keep their irreversible outcome paths. A crash before or after the
boundary recovers conservatively as submit_unknown without resending.

Disconnect can remove the private draft before a pending attempt is settled.
That recovery locks and rechecks the exact owner, posting, email ATS and active
mail_send_id. Wrong owner, posting and send ID neighbours cannot change the
application. The source does not reconstruct private mail content.

B3: Email subject contract
Email subjects reject CR or LF and more than 998 codepoints before answer
mutation. Legacy invalid subjects return a typed 422 on approval and remain
editable. Draft synchronisation validates before changing the draft. Approval
views expose max_chars=998, and the frontend parser accepts that exact limit.
The existing editor uses its native UTF16 count and displays the server error.
The ordinary 5000 and document-letter 10000 limits remain in place.
