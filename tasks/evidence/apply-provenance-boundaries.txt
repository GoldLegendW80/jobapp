Change
  A forward token iterator retains a complete quoted name as one opaque span,
  including LF, CRLF, CR and word apostrophes. An unmatched or mismatched span
  provides no claim sentences. Each token is consumed once by this quote pass.
  Typed employment and education rows live in an Apply-local metadata field.
  They still support independent entity, number and date fact checking, but
  cannot prove a personal achievement or preference merely by containing it.
  FactBlock bodies, motivation, permitted answers and the immutable prepared
  letter remain personal prose sources. FactBlock titles are not prose sources.
  The shared mail Sources type and recorded model streams are unchanged.
