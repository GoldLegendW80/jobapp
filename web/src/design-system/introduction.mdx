# jobapp design system

A calm workspace for a job search. A grey canvas, white cards with soft corners, near-black pills
for the actions, and one orange for what matters: the figure to look at first, a chart, progress,
a count that waits for you. The postings, CVs and applications are the content; the interface stays quiet
around them.

## Principles

1. **One black pill per group.** The primary action (`primary`) is a near-black pill; the actions
   beside it are grey pills (`secondary`) or quiet (`ghost`). The active section of a navigation
   is a black pill too.
2. **Orange is rare.** `brand` marks the featured figure, charts, progress and the counts that
   wait for the person. Never an ordinary button. Focus is a neutral ring, so it never reads as
   an error.
3. **Words first, color second.** Fit, status and pay are always said in words; their tone only
   helps the eye. Meaning colors (`success`, `warning`, `info`, `destructive`) carry meaning,
   never categories.
4. **White on grey is the depth.** Cards sit on the canvas without a border or a shadow; panels
   inside a card are grey. Only menus, popovers and dialogs cast a shadow.
5. **Soft and round.** Every action is a pill; fields 12 px, cards 20 px, dialogs 24 px.
6. **Motion answers a click.** Feedback in 120 ms, overlays under 250 ms, one strong ease out,
   nothing that moves by itself.

## How it is built

- **Tokens** live in one file, `web/src/index.css`: color, radius, shadow, motion and the type
  family (Instrument Sans). The app and this Storybook read the same file; change a token and
  both follow.
- **Components** are the shadcn components of `web/src/components/ui`, each with its story next
  to it, plus a few shared blocks in `web/src/components` (PageHeader, PillNav, Brand).
- **Pages** are only compositions of those components; `eslint.config.js` forbids a hand-built
  button, field, table or dialog. Under **Pages**, every route of the app renders for real (its
  guards, the shell, the page), against the same fake api as the tests. Each story is one user
  story: "As a job seeker, I want ..., so that ...".
- **Themes**: the paint brush in the toolbar switches between the system setting, light and
  dark. The app follows the system setting.

## Writing in the interface

Plain words from the person's side ("Find jobs", not "Run a sweep"); the same verb from the button
to the toast ("Apply", then "Application sent"); sentence case; errors say what happened and what
to do next. No dash as a separator in any text a person reads: use a comma, a colon or a new
sentence.
